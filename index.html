Jaguars On SI • John Shipley • Training-camp winners & losers

Branson named a training-camp WINNER and “key role player” for the defense

🔥 DIRECT BRANSON MENTION CONFIRMED

John Shipley lists Branson Combs among Jacksonville’s training-camp winners. He says he does not expect a single clear winner between Combs and Ventrell Miller for the linebacker spot opposite Foye Oluokun, but calls that outcome a win for Branson. Shipley’s key conclusion is that Combs leaves camp as a key role player for the defense, noting that he could have slipped back into a special-teams-only role but never did.

Family takeaway: This is one of the clearest independent end-of-camp evaluations yet: Branson maintained and expanded his defensive role throughout camp rather than simply surviving the competition.
