Branson logged 15 defensive snaps and an 84.3 overall PFF grade in Week 1

🔥 DIRECT BRANSON DATA CONFIRMED

PFF’s player page, updated after Jacksonville’s opener, credits Branson with 15 defensive snaps: eight against the run, six in coverage and one as a pass rusher. His overall defensive grade is 84.3, with a 74.6 run-defense grade, 73.0 pass-rush grade and 68.6 coverage grade. PFF also records one pressure — the sack — no missed tackles and a 0.0 passer rating allowed when targeted. The sample is small, so PFF does not rank him among qualifying linebackers yet.

Family takeaway: The encouraging part is not only the sack. Jacksonville used Branson in run defense, coverage and as a blitzer, and his first regular-season defensive workload graded very well overall. Week 2 at Denver will show whether those 15 snaps expand.
