🔥 BRANSON MAKES THE JAGUARS’ INITIAL 53-MAN ROSTER

🔥 DIRECT BRANSON ROSTER CONFIRMATION

Jacksonville officially reduced its roster to 53 players and Branson Combs is one of only five linebackers retained. The official Jaguars roster lists #50 Branson Combs alongside Foye Oluokun, Ventrell Miller, Dennis Gardeck and Jahlani Tavai. The club released or waived several other linebackers, including Jack Kiser, Yasir Abdullah, Jalen McLeod and Baylon Spector.

Family takeaway: This is the clearest milestone of camp. Branson did not merely survive as a fringe depth player — he made the initial 53 in a linebacker room the Jaguars trimmed all the way down to five. Combined with Liam Coen’s statement that Combs and Miller will both play, this strongly supports Branson entering the regular season with a real defensive role.
