NEW TONIGHT — Coen says both Combs and Miller will play defensive snaps this season

🔥 DIRECT BRANSON MENTION CONFIRMED

John Shipley’s Day 19 joint-practice report directly revisits the Jaguars’ linebacker competition. Shipley reports that Liam Coen made it clear after the Carolina preseason game that both Ventrell Miller and Branson Combs will see defensive snaps this year regardless of which player technically starts.

The Aug. 25 practice offered another clue about how the workload may begin: Shipley observed that Miller appeared to receive the most reps alongside Foyesade Oluokun and praised Miller’s downhill run defense. That is a point in Miller’s favor for the nominal starting spot, but the more important development for Branson is Coen’s stated plan to use both linebackers rather than treating this as a winner-take-all competition.

Family takeaway: Branson’s role no longer appears dependent on winning the word “starter” on the depth chart. Coen has publicly indicated that both players have earned meaningful defensive work. Today Miller appeared to get more first-unit reps, so tomorrow’s second joint practice with Tampa Bay is worth watching closely.
